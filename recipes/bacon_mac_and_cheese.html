Bacon Mac & Cheese Recipe

Bacon Macaroni and Cheese Coming Right Up!

This is a delicious variation of macaroni and cheese made with penne pasta, bacon, and garlic. The secret to the cheese sauce is constant stirring over low heat. Add the milk just a bit at a time and keep stirring

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bacon
  • 8 ounces penne pasta
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
